## RCSB Protein Data Bank structure data

- Local files: `assets/structures/structures.js` and `assets/structures/*.pdb`
- Source: https://www.rcsb.org/
- License / terms: RCSB PDB data are released into the public domain (CC0 1.0) —
  https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
- Included examples:
  - `1BNA` — Drew–Dickerson B-DNA dodecamer (CGCGAATTCGCG)
  - `1EHZ` — tRNA-Phe
  - `1L2Y` — Trp-cage miniprotein (NMR)
  - `1GFL` — green fluorescent protein
  - `2HHB` — deoxyhemoglobin
- Processing: `tools/pdb2json.py` removes water molecules and hydrogens, keeps heavy atoms for
  small structures and Cα traces for large proteins, rounds coordinates to 0.01 Å, and stores the
  result as compact JSON. No coordinates were modified or modeled by the project.
- Use: local educational visualization only; each structure card links to its RCSB record.

## Fonts

The application uses system font stacks declared in `index.html` and does not load a third-party web-font stylesheet at runtime. If a future release bundles font files, add their source and license here before publication.

## Original work

The HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icon artwork, and service worker in this repository are original project work. All bioinformatics calculations (reverse complement, translation, ORF finding, restriction
digest, pairwise alignment, nearest-neighbour Tm, primer scoring, GenBank generation) and the
three-dimensional molecular geometry are implemented in this project. No user sequence is sent to a
third-party service by the application logic.
